# Dayline Terms of Service (Draft)

Last updated: 2026-08-31

**This is a working draft prepared for a private beta. It is not a
finalized legal document and has not been reviewed by a lawyer — see
`docs/OWNER_ACTIONS_REQUIRED.md`.** It is kept byte-identical to the
in-app copy at `mobile/src/constants/legalText.ts` (`TERMS_TEXT`) so the
app and this document can never silently drift apart — edit that file and
copy the change here (or vice versa) rather than editing just one.

---

1. **What Dayline is.** Dayline lets you capture short daily video clips,
   turn them into a private montage, and optionally share that montage
   with a small group you choose (up to 10 people). Dayline is not a
   public social network — content you create is private by default.

2. **Eligibility.** You must be at least 13 years old to use Dayline. By
   creating an account you confirm you meet this requirement.

3. **Your content.** You own the clips and montages you create. You grant
   Dayline a limited license to store, process, and transmit your content
   solely to provide the service (e.g., rendering your montage,
   delivering it to a group you chose to share it with).

4. **Groups.** Content you explicitly share to a group becomes visible to
   that group's current members until you remove it, leave, or the group
   is deleted. Removing a member or leaving a group revokes future access
   immediately.

5. **Acceptable use.** No harassment, no illegal content, no
   impersonation, no attempting to access another user's private content.
   See `COMMUNITY_RULES.md` for details.

6. **Termination.** You may delete your account at any time from
   Settings. We may suspend accounts that violate these terms or the
   Community Rules.

7. **Disclaimers.** The service is provided "as is" during this beta.
   Features, availability, and pricing may change.

8. **Contact.** support@dayline.app
