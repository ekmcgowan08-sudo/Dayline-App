## Audio

Dayline does not bundle any music or sound effects. The only audio in any
Dayline-produced video is:
1. **Original audio recorded by the user** as part of their own 5-second
   clips (their content, their rights).
2. **Synthesized silence** (`anullsrc`, generated by ffmpeg itself, not a
   licensed asset) for clips that had no audio track, so montage segments
   have consistent stream layouts — see `worker/src/render/pipeline.ts`.

No third-party or commercial music is used anywhere in the rendering
pipeline, consistent with the product requirement ("No copyrighted
commercial music... use only original audio or audio with documented
redistribution rights"). If a future version adds a licensed music
catalog (e.g., Epidemic Sound, Artlist — see `COSTS.md`'s note on this),
add it to this table with its license terms before shipping.
